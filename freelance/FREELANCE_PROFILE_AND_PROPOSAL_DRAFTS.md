I help turn spreadsheets and business data into clear reports that are easier to understand and use.

My project-based work focuses on:
- Excel data cleaning, formatting, PivotTables, formulas, and reporting.
- Power BI dashboards, KPI definitions, data modeling, and DAX measures.

My workflow is straightforward: understand the business question, inspect the available data, agree on scope and KPI definitions, document data-cleaning decisions, analyze the results, and deliver organized files with a short handover.

I am building my professional track record through practical portfolio projects. I do not claim client results or business improvements that have not been independently verified.

For a useful estimate, please share the type of file, approximate row count, required KPIs, and the decision the report should support.
